PhD research proposal

Evidence-grounded multi-agent LLM decision making in financial markets

The big question: if AI agents are given solid numerical forecasts and clearly defined evidence, and are made to challenge each other before deciding, do they make better trading decisions than simpler approaches? And is any improvement worth what it costs?
